## Problem

Gilgit-Baltistan is one of the most spectacular regions in the world, but travel
information is scattered, unreliable, or written for mass tourism rather than
independent travellers. Visitors have no single source they can ask specific
questions: which valleys are reachable in four days, what is the budget for two
people, which food is local rather than tourist-facing.

## Who it is for

Independent travellers and small groups planning a trip to Gilgit-Baltistan who
want specific, honest answers rather than generic travel-blog content.

## What I built

An AI trip planner and RAG chatbot. Users enter how many days they have, their
budget, their destination within GB, and their interests (nature, culture, food,
adventure). The chatbot generates a day-by-day itinerary and answers follow-up
questions from a knowledge base built on Rohail's original research about the
region — places, food, and hidden valleys that do not appear in standard guides.

## Stack

- **Next.js** — frontend and API routes
- **Node.js** — server logic
- **Gemini API** — generation
- **RAG** — retrieval-augmented generation from original research
- **Vercel** — deployment

## Key decisions

**Why RAG instead of a fine-tuned model?** The knowledge base needs to be updated
as new information about GB travel becomes available. RAG lets me add or edit
documents without retraining. It also keeps the chatbot honest: it only answers
from the indexed content rather than hallucinating from general training data.

**Why require sign-in?** A RAG chatbot with a live LLM backend has real API costs
per message. Sign-in lets me identify heavy users and rate-limit fairly.
